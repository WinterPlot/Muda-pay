# D-U-N-S number checklist (needed before publishing MudaPay on Google Play)

Google Play requires a **D-U-N-S number** to create an **Organization** developer account for Userlab Ltd. It is free; do not pay anyone for it.

## ❓ Have you finished your D&B (Dun & Bradstreet) D-U-N-S request?

Answer honestly, then follow the matching path:

- **No, I have not started** -> do steps 1 to 5 below.
- **Yes, I submitted it and I am waiting** -> do step 4 (answer D&B's verification call or email) and wait. It can take up to 30 days.
- **Yes, I received my D-U-N-S number** -> go to "When you have the number" at the end.

## Steps

- [ ] **1. Check if Userlab Ltd already has a number.** Open dnb.com, find the D-U-N-S lookup, search the company. If it is listed, you already have it: check that the name and address are correct and skip to step 5.
- [ ] **2. Request one.** On the D&B "Get a D-U-N-S Number" page choose: your business type (US, Canada or **International** = where Userlab Ltd is registered), then **"I'm a Google developer"**. For Africa / South Asia / Middle East D&B sends you to **dnbsame.com**; open it from the link on dnb.com, never from an ad or a message.
- [ ] **3. Fill in the form, copied from your company registration certificate:**
  - Legal name (exactly as registered, including "Ltd")
  - Business address (physical address, no PO box)
  - Mailing address
  - Contact number (with country code, answered during working hours)
  - Contact name and title (the director or CEO)
  - Number of employees at that address
- [ ] **4. Answer D&B's verification call or email quickly.** Keep your registration certificate at hand.
- [ ] **5. Receive the 9-digit number by email** and save it somewhere safe.

## Warnings

- The D-U-N-S number is **free**. People pretend to be D&B and sell it: never pay someone who contacts you first. D&B's own optional "expedited" service is the only paid option.
- Apply as **Userlab Ltd**, not as yourself personally.
- The name and address at D&B must be **identical** to your legal registration and to your Google payments profile. If D&B shows old details, ask them to correct them before creating the Play account.
- If your country is not covered by D&B, contact Google Play support **before** creating the developer account to ask for the alternative verification.

## When you have the number

1. Create the Google payments profile for Userlab Ltd (same legal name and address as D&B).
2. Create the Play Console **Organization** account and enter the D-U-N-S number.
3. Verify the contact and developer email and phone (one-time codes), and finish developer identity verification.
4. Continue with `PLAY_PUBLISHING.md`.
